# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two overlapping audiences arriving at a personal site (ace139.github.io):

- **Peers and the technically curious** (primary): engineers, founders, product leaders, and AI/ML practitioners who find a post via search or social and want to gauge whether Soumyo thinks rigorously and builds real things. They read in depth, skim for substance, and judge credibility fast.
- **People evaluating Soumyo professionally** (secondary): potential collaborators, hires, or clients who've encountered his work (sometimes through Oogway Labs) and want to size up the person behind it.

Context of use: mostly self-directed reading (desktop and mobile), often mid-scroll from a link. The job to be done is *"decide whether this person is worth following, trusting, hiring, or reading,"* then read the piece without friction.

## Product Purpose

A personal brand home and long-form publishing surface for **Soumyo Dey**, a builder and systems thinker who has spent the past decade shipping products and platforms across AI, data, systems, and product, and who writes about what holds up in the real world.

It exists to establish him as a credible operator and thinker **in his own right**, not as a billboard for any one company. The site reflects the breadth of his work and interests, which extend beyond his current role.

Soumyo is currently Founder & CEO of [Oogway Labs](https://oogwaylabs.com/) (an AI-native consulting and engineering firm). That is a present position, surfaced where it's relevant (the About page, the résumé, the writing), but **not** the homepage headline or the site's purpose. The writing is the proof: evidence-led pieces on building things that work. Success looks like a reader finishing a post convinced he knows the systems deeply, then following, subscribing, or reaching out.

(The earlier Cornet Health / dentist-burnout content belongs to a closed chapter and is being retired.)

## Brand Personality

**Authoritative, direct, and quietly funny.** The voice of a senior practitioner who explains hard-won understanding in plain language and grounds every claim in shipped work, never aspiration, never buzzwords. It mirrors Oogway's own register: confident without hype, precise about what actually works in production, and willing to name what breaks at 3am. The default mode is measured and editorial, with the occasional dry aside that signals a human, not a marketing department. It earns trust by reasoning in the open and by showing receipts, not by selling.

Three words: **grounded, direct, wry.**

## Anti-references

- **AI-agency / consultancy hype** (the headline one): no "AI transformation partner" buzzword soup, no vague capability claims, no overpromising. Oogway's whole stance is execution over hype; the personal site must never undercut that by sounding like the agencies it's better than.
- **Generic SaaS landing pages**: no hero-metric templates, no identical icon-heading-text feature-card grids, no gradient-on-navy startup look.
- **AI-slop / templated output**: no tracked uppercase eyebrow on every section, no cream/paper "editorial-default" body background, none of the generic sameness that reads as machine-made.
- **Corporate / clinical sterility**: no sterile blue-and-white brochure tone, no stock-photo gloss, no soulless enterprise polish.
- **Loud hype marketing**: no exclamation-heavy CTAs, no growth-hack conversion-bait.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over promise.** Every claim is grounded in shipped work. Show the receipts; don't assert capability, demonstrate it.
2. **Practitioner, not pitch.** The site sells credibility by being credible, not by marketing. It reads as a builder sharing understanding; when in doubt, remove the marketing move.
3. **Earn trust by reasoning in the open.** Substance and evidence carry the design; the layout serves the argument rather than decorating it. Show the thinking, don't just state the conclusion.
4. **Distinct, never templated.** Actively avoid the category-reflex look: AI-agency, generic SaaS, AI-default sameness. If the design could be guessed from the genre alone, push it further.
5. **Reading is the product.** Line length, contrast, hierarchy, and motion all answer one question: does this make the writing easier and more compelling to read?

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA**. Concretely: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (large/bold text ≥3:1), full keyboard navigation, semantic landmark structure, and visible focus states. Both dark (default) and light themes must clear the bar. Every load-in / scroll-driven animation needs a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative (crossfade or instant), and content must never be gated behind a reveal that fails to fire in reduced-motion or headless contexts.
